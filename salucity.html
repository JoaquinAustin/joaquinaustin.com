Overview

Salucity allows users to book online, in-person, or at-home appointments with nearby healthcare specialists and doctors. It started as a website, and thanks to its success, we've developed a native app to offer better features and further grow the platform.

Role
Product Designer @ Digitup Studio

Year
2022

Context

The Salucity website has seen strong adoption in its early years, with over 800 active users per month. User interviews conducted to better understand their needs revealed that more than 74% wanted a mobile app to support key tasks, such as receiving timely notifications and managing appointments within their existing calendars and schedules. In response to these insights, the app was designed to extend the web experience and reduce friction in users' daily workflows.

Key insights from research

Since many users schedule appointments for others—such as minors or elderly relatives—supporting multiple profiles within a single account is essential.

The application must integrate seamlessly with other tools, such as calendars and scheduling apps.

A rating system helps build trust and transparency between patients and professionals.

Keeping communication within the app allows both parties to maintain a clear record of the entire contact and service process.

Finding solutions

The application serves two types of users: end customers and professionals. Solutions must be designed to address the distinct needs, goals, and workflows of each user group.

For end customers

On one hand, the app for end users, who discover, book, and manage services provided by professionals. Professional discovery feature that allows users to easily search and explore available professionals based on their needs. Users can browse profiles, compare services, view ratings and availability.

My Appointments

This section provides users with a clear overview of their scheduled, upcoming, and completed appointments, making it easy to manage bookings and stay organized.

Multiple users under a single profile

The app allows users to manage multiple profiles within a single account, enabling them to book and organize appointments for themselves and others.

For professionals

For professionals, the app provides tools to manage and organize appointments efficiently, including the ability to suggest alternative dates and time slots. A professional profile where users can set available days and time slots, as well as add blockers to manage unavailability.

Design system

The product is built on a scalable design system, ensuring consistency across the platform while allowing for future expansion and new features.

Business impact

63%

Of professionals appreciate the ability to suggest new appointments, reducing the effort previously needed via messages or phone calls.

57%

Of web users have transitioned to the mobile app.

Notifications

From user interviews, we learned that notifications encourage users to book more sessions and services.

Conclusions

Designing an app to streamline hiring professionals has been quite a challenge — there was a lot of ground to cover, and thanks to the research, we were able to uncover many situations we hadn't anticipated. One important finding is that many users still prefer using the website over the app, especially older users, so the need to bring certain app functionalities to the website has come up as an improvement priority for the near future.

Note: some data here has been adjusted for confidentiality and may not be fully exact.
