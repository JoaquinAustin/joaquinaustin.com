JMA

Car insurance is one of the company's flagship products, and its online calculator is one of the most requested tools by users. For the past two years, I've been leading the Car Calculator product alongside the business team, constantly working to optimize the user experience and increase booking conversions.

Role
Lead Product Designer

Year
Ongoing since 2023

Only Visuals

Context

Despite having a very high volume of users, the dropout rate was above normal levels. From a product perspective, pricing and coverage were competitive and aligned with market standards. From a technological standpoint, everything was functioning correctly. We therefore focused on data analysis and user interviews to understand the reasons behind the high dropout rate.

Framing the problem

29%

Of users drop off after the second step. Most of them believe that there are many things they have to answer before they can see the price.

43%

Of users drop at the packages view. Once they reach the product offering, they find it difficult to understand the product.

Time

Average users take between 2 and 3 weeks to decide on a car insurance, often repeatedly comparing different companies.

Finding solutions

We focused on the user flow to reach a meaningful price and ensure it doesn't change abruptly as we learn more about the user. Close collaboration with business and legal teams was key to understanding the minimum information required. As a result, we reduced the flow by one step and enabled users to reach the price earlier in the journey.

The outcomes

New product offer and customization

The new table allows users to compare prices and coverage, but also to customize them, as well as the add-ons, all in one place. Previously, customization had to be done within each option, making the comparison process lengthy and confusing.

Save for later feature

We implemented a new feature that allows users to save their progress at any point in the flow. By leaving their email address, they can return via a link and continue exactly where they left off.

New dynamic panel to dive deeper into coverages and for getting in touch

We wanted to explain coverages in a more user-friendly way. Instead of using tooltips, we designed a new overlay panel where users can access real-life examples and short videos that clarify key concepts. When needed, they can contact an agent at any time or book an appointment for personalized advice.

New final screen

The updated end screen of the process helps users understand what they've purchased and the next steps to take.

Business impact

+11% direct conversion

The majority of users were very satisfied with the table that allows them to compare and customize everything in one place.

Save for Later

17% of users engaged with the "Save for later" feature. 4% of them converted later.

8%

Of users booked appointments using the calendar feature. The introduction of new contact options has improved lead retention and driven higher sales closure.

To improve

Technical explanations remain confusing; we'll keep collaborating with legal to make the product as user-friendly and understandable as possible.

Design overview

Note: due to information sensitivity, some of the data shown here isn't 100% exact, and not all processes or screens are displayed. It's also worth mentioning that improving metrics (or sometimes not hitting targets) isn't just on the Product team. It takes all teams working together to make the experience better and get the best results.
